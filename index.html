What this is

We signed up for Billdu like a new user, found your paywall, and held it up against the report.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and State of Paywalls 2026 is what we learned from 78,000+ of their paywalls. We installed Billdu, signed in with Apple, named a business, and found Choose a plan under Settings → Subscription → Subscribe. Then we compared it with the report, element by element, and rebuilt it as a clickable screen in your design.

Benchmarks are for all categories in Europe, your largest App Store region. The report covers eight categories and Business is not one of them, so we never borrow a neighbouring category’s numbers.

We did not redesign your paywall

The proposed screen is yours: your Monthly / Annually switch, your three-card carousel, your line illustrations, your Popular ribbon, your price buttons and your prices. We added a few lines you already have the facts for.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If you have already run one of these, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest.

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Each one opens the prototype on the screen that shows it.

01

Catch the close with one more offer

Tap X on Choose a plan and you land back on the Subscription page, with a Subscribe button and nothing else. A “not now” ends the conversation. Offer your own Lite plan at 4,99 € a month instead, and remind them their trial is still running.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 4% of apps show another paywall after close. It converts at 0.88× the main one and beats it in 45% of apps.
See the screen ARPU +10–15%Based on Adapty data
02

Tell them where they are in their trial

Every new account gets 7 days of Premium, but the paywall never says so. The end date sits on a different screen. Put “day 1 of 7” and the expiry date above the plans, and “Cancel anytime” under the button. You already say “cancel anytime” in your App Store listing.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 (Europe) · 18.4% of trials convert to paid, against 26.2% in the US.
See the screen ARPU +12–36%Observed in winning A/B tests
03

Put your 4.8 stars next to the price

Your banner says “Join thousands of happy businesses!” with no number behind it. You have 4.8 stars from 26,000+ ratings on the US App Store, and reviews like “Fast, easy to use, affordable.” Put both on the paywall, in the banner you already have.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 27% of top paywalls show social proof.
See the screen CR +10–30%Observed in winning A/B tests
04

Write the headline for the person reading it

Sign in with Apple can share a first name, and onboarding asks for the business name. The paywall still opens on “Choose a plan”, the same three words for everyone. Use what you know, plus their trial end date.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 2% of top paywalls use what the app knows about the user in the headline.

Potential outcome

All four work on the moment someone is already deciding: who the paywall speaks to, what it says about the trial they are in, what proves it, and what happens when they say no.
